Will I feel high?

No. Every EMBR product stays under the legal THC threshold, so you get the calming effect of the plant without the intoxication.

Time to feel it 10–20 min, sublingual

How fast does it work?

Drops under the tongue land in 10 to 20 minutes. The mist is almost instant. The roll builds slowly over the first hour, made to taper you into sleep rather than switch it off.

Starting dose 5 drops or 1 spray

How do I find my dose?

Start there, then wait 45 minutes before adjusting. Dose depends on body weight and what you're treating, and the guide in every box walks through both.
